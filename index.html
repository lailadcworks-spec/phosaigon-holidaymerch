1

Executive summary

Greenfield Development Corporation has announced mall-wide marketing initiatives for October to December 2026. This proposal recommends a selective, sales-focused approach for Phở Saigon at Paseo Outlets. Rather than joining every event, we will prioritize activities that can support restaurant traffic, increase average transaction value, and provide useful brand exposure.

The proposed campaign, Shop, Dine & Unwind, positions Phở Saigon as a convenient dining stop for shoppers, families, and groups. The plan uses mall-led exposure and our existing social channels, with a small budget reserved for approved promotional materials and one limited-time meal bundle. No blanket menu discounts are proposed.

2

Objectives

  • Encourage more dine-in visits during key shopping and holiday dates.
  • Increase average transaction value through a carefully costed meal bundle.
  • Make the most of available mall promotions and content production opportunities.
  • Keep execution practical for the branch team and the marketing workload.
  • Measure results to decide whether the campaign should be repeated.

Campaign concept

Core message

Make Phở Saigon part of your Paseo Outlets day. Shop for your favorites, enjoy the holiday atmosphere, then take a break over Vietnamese comfort food with family and friends.

Content directions

  • Black Friday

    More savings, more satisfying meals

    Promote the approved limited-time bundle.

  • Christmas

    Gather around the table

    Feature sharing dishes and family or group dining.

  • Shopping break

    Your phở break at Paseo

    Show popular dishes and the restaurant as an easy stop during a shopping trip.

Use existing food photos and videos wherever possible. Keep posts location-specific and include a clear invitation to dine in.

5

Proposed Black Friday meal bundle

Nov 27–29

One limited-time bundle, subject to management approval and food cost review. The goal is a clear value offer that protects menu margins, not a discount on the whole menu.

Suggested mechanics

  1. Choose a combination of selected mains with a drink or side that is simple for the kitchen and service team to execute.
  2. Set the bundle price only after checking ingredient costs, current menu prices, and expected contribution margin.
  3. Limit the offer to selected items and the stated dates. Exclude other promotions unless management approves stacking.
  4. Use a simple POS note, tally sheet, or redemption code to track bundle sales.
  5. Submit the final approved product images, regular prices, and bundle price to the mall by its confirmed deadline.

Optional Christmas dining feature

Instead of launching another discount, feature existing sharing dishes or group-friendly combinations in holiday content. A second bundle should only be introduced if the first offer works well operationally and the numbers support it.

Measurement

  • Number of bundle orders and total bundle sales.
  • Average transaction value and transaction count compared with a suitable baseline.
  • Sales of the selected items before and during the offer, where data is available.
  • Social media reach, engagement, video views, and profile actions.
  • Any reach or engagement data shared by the mall.
  • Branch feedback on preparation time, service flow, and customer response.

After the campaign, Marketing will prepare a short recap with results, lessons learned, and a recommendation on whether to repeat or adjust the offer.
