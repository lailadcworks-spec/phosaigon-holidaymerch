What's on the wrap

Every small picture is something from Vietnam, so the mug doubles as a conversation starter.

  • PhởNoodle soup

    Rice noodles in a slow, fragrant broth with fresh herbs. The bowl our name comes from.

  • Bánh mìVietnamese baguette

    Crisp bread, pâté, pickled vegetables, chili and herbs.

  • Cà phê sữa đáIced milk coffee

    Strong dark coffee over condensed milk and ice.

  • Nón láLeaf hat

    The conical palm-leaf hat worn against sun and rain.

  • Hoa maiYellow blossoms

    Apricot blossoms that fill homes in the south during Tết, the Lunar New Year.

  • Đèn lồngLanterns

    Red lanterns hung for celebrations and old-town nights.

  • Hạ LongThe bay

    Limestone islands rising out of green water, with a junk boat sailing past.

  • GheRiver boat

    A rower on the water, a scene from life along Vietnam's rivers.

  • Xe đạpBicycle

    Still one of the easiest ways to get around a Vietnamese street.

  • Phố cổOld town houses

    Shuttered windows, balconies and tiled roofs, like the old quarters in Hội An.

  • RồngDragon

    A symbol of strength and good fortune in temples and festivals.

  • Hoa senLotus

    Often called Vietnam's national flower. It grows from muddy ponds and still blooms clean.

Before we produce

Still to confirm

Nothing here is final yet. These are the decisions we need before anything goes public.

  • Prices

    Selling price for the mug, each pin or the pin set, and the tee.

  • Quantities and supplier

    How many of each to order, and lead time so stock arrives before the holidays.

  • Launch date

    When the merch goes on sale in branches.

  • Stock per branch

    Whether all four branches carry every item and every mug color.

  • Tee sizes and fabric

    Size run, fabric weight and whether there is a front print.

  • Pins: set or singles

    Sold as a set of six, one by one, or both.

  • "PHO" vs "PHỞ" on the tee

    The tee prints PHO without the accent while our logo uses PHỞ. Decide which one we want.

  • Clean the cream mug file

    The cream mockup shows a faint extra logo near the iced coffee and near the lanterns. Make sure it's not in the print file.

  • Passport rules

    Whether stamps count at all four branches, any minimum spend per stamp, and when the card expires.

  • 10% off with other promos

    Whether the 7th-visit discount covers the whole bill and whether it can be combined with offers like the Black Friday bundle.
